OpenEscrow

A simple on-chain escrow for safe freelance payments.

Problem

Freelancers and clients often rely on middlemen to handle payments. This creates trust issues, delays, and extra fees.

Solution

OpenEscrow lets clients deposit funds into a smart contract. The funds are released only when the work is approved.

How it works

Client deposits funds → Freelancer completes work → Client approves → Smart contract releases payment.

Goal

To make freelance payments safer, more transparent, and trust-minimized through open finance infrastructure.
