# OpenEscrow

OpenEscrow is a simple on-chain escrow demo for safe freelance payments.

## Problem

Freelancers and clients often need a trusted middleman to handle payments. This creates delays, extra fees, and trust issues.

## Solution

OpenEscrow uses a smart contract to hold funds safely until the work is approved.

## How it works

1. The client creates a job.
2. The client deposits funds into the escrow contract.
3. The freelancer completes the work.
4. The client approves the result.
5. The contract releases funds to the freelancer.

## Why it matters

OpenEscrow brings safer, transparent, and trust-minimized payments to open finance.
